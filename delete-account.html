Delete your Ideal Near account

Ideal Near is published by Ideal Network. You can delete your Ideal Near account and its data at any time.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Ideal Near and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom right).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — without the app

Email idealnetwork2021@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Ideal Near, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address)
  • Your name shown on reviews
  • Businesses you listed, with their photos, opening hours and deals
  • Requests you posted and offers you sent or received
  • Reviews you wrote (business ratings are recalculated)
  • Bookings, including the phone number you gave for them
  • Your chats and messages
  • Your saved places, reports and blocks
  • Notification registrations for your phones

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records for Featured or Pro plans you bought (amount, date, order number and business name) are kept for up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting rules. They are not linked to your account after deletion.
  • Google Play subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel Pro in the Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Anonymous statistics (for example, how many times a business was viewed) are kept, because they cannot identify you.
  • Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
