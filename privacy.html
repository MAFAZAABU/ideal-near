Ideal Near 📍

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Ideal Near ("the app", "we") helps people find businesses, products, services, deals and professionals near them, and lets businesses list themselves. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Operated by: Ideal Network, India
Contact: idealnetwork2021@gmail.com

Information we collect

Account

  • Your email address, used to sign you in — either with Google, or with a one-time link or code we email you.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • The name you choose to show on reviews, bookings and chats.
  • Your chosen app language (English or Tamil), so notifications use it.

Location

  • Your phone's location is used on your device to show what is within 5 km and to sort results by distance. It is sent to our server only as part of a search, and is not stored.
  • When you post a request ("Tell us what you need"), its location is stored rounded to about 100 metres so your exact address is not kept.
  • When you add a listing, the location you pin is stored and shown publicly, because customers need to find the business.

Things you create

  • Listings, photos and opening hours you publish are public.
  • Reviews are public and show your chosen name.
  • Requests are shown to businesses within 5 km without your name or email.
  • Offers a business sends are shown to the customer who made the request, with the business's name and phone number.
  • Bookings: your chosen name, the phone number you enter, the time and your note are shared only with that business.
  • Chat messages between you and a business are stored so both of you can read them, and are visible only to the two of you (and to our moderators if a message is reported).
  • Saved places are stored in your account so they are kept across devices.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a listing, review, user or message, we store the report and the reason so we can review it. If you block someone, we store that so they cannot message you.

Business statistics

  • We count how often each listing is shown, opened, saved, and how often its Call, WhatsApp and Directions buttons are tapped, so the business owner can see how their listing performs. These counts do not show who tapped.

Payments

  • Businesses can buy Featured or Pro plans. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. We receive and keep the order number, product, date, amount and the listing it was bought for.

Device

  • A push notification token for your phone, so we can notify you about requests, offers, bookings and messages. It is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

We do not show advertising, sell your data, or use third-party analytics.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo access only when you add photos to a listing. Only the photos you choose are uploaded, and they are shown publicly on that listing.

Services we use

  • Supabase — hosts the database, sign-in, chat and photo storage.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in with your Google account.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications.
  • Google Play Billing — processes payments for Featured and Pro plans.
  • Brevo — delivers sign-in emails.
  • MapTiler (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors) — your phone downloads map images from their servers, which see your IP address like any website.
  • Phone, WhatsApp, Google Maps and share — open only when you tap Call, WhatsApp, Directions or Share, and are then covered by those apps' own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, listings and photos, requests and offers, reviews, bookings, chats, saved places and notification tokens. See Delete your account for details, including what is kept.

To request deletion without the app, email idealnetwork2021@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure users can only see and change their own listings, reviews, requests, bookings and chats.

Children

Ideal Near is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify users in the app.
